At Pockeva, your privacy matters. Pockeva is designed to help you manage your personal finances while keeping your financial information under your control. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

01

Information Collection

Pockeva does not collect, transmit, or store your personal or financial information on servers operated by the developer. The app does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or submit personal information to use its core features.

02

Local Data Storage

Information you enter into Pockeva is stored locally on your device. This may include:

  • Income and expense records.
  • Wallet information and balances.
  • Transaction categories and history.
  • Shopping lists and reminders.
  • Loan, lending, and borrowing records.
  • Budgets and financial reports.

Your locally stored information remains under your control.

03

Internet Connectivity

Pockeva is designed to work offline, and its core financial management features do not require an internet connection. Your financial records are not uploaded to Pockeva servers.

04

Data Sharing

Pockeva does not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal or financial records with third parties. The developer does not receive your locally stored financial records through the app.

05

Advertising and Analytics

Pockeva does not use third-party advertising services or behavioral advertising trackers. It is not designed to build advertising profiles or track your financial activity across other apps and services.

06

Backup and Export

Pockeva may provide local backup and export features so you can keep a copy of your information. Any backup or exported file is generated for your personal use.

You are responsible for where you store these files and with whom you share them. If you move a backup to another device or an external storage or cloud service, that transfer is outside Pockeva's control.

07

Data Security

Your financial records are stored locally on your device. Their security also depends on your device's operating system, security settings, and physical access controls. We recommend using a secure screen lock and keeping your backups in a safe location.

08

Data Retention and Deletion

Your information remains on your device until you delete it, clear the application's data, or uninstall Pockeva. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may permanently remove locally stored financial records.

We recommend creating a backup before clearing app data or uninstalling the application. The developer does not maintain copies of your financial records on external servers.

09

Children's Privacy

Pockeva is a general-purpose personal finance application and is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application, its features, or applicable requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Please review this page periodically.

Questions about your privacy?

If you have questions, concerns, or feedback about this Privacy Policy or Pockeva's privacy practices, get in touch.
