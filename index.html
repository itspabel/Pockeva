Features

Everything you need to stay in balance

Pockeva keeps day-to-day money organized — no account, no cloud, no internet connection required.

Income & Expense Tracking

Record and organize everyday transactions with the details that matter to you.

Wallet Management

Manage cash, mobile financial services, bank accounts and custom wallets as records inside the app.

Shopping List

Organize shopping items, quantities, units, purchase costs and a history of past shopping.

Borrowing & Lending

Keep track of money you have borrowed and money you have lent to others.

Financial Insights

Review financial summaries, spending patterns and reports generated from your own records.

PDF Reports

Generate financial reports for personal reference, created locally on your device.

Local Backup & Restore

Export and restore supported app data using local backup files you control.

Offline-First Experience

Designed for managing personal finances without requiring an online account.

Personalization

Profile settings and light/dark appearance options to make the app feel like yours.

Privacy

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Pockeva?

Pockeva is an offline-first personal finance app for Android. It helps you record income and expenses, manage wallets, keep shopping lists, track borrowing and lending, and review financial summaries — all in one organized place.

Is Pockeva available on Google Play?

No. Pockeva is distributed as an Android APK through GitHub Releases. The release page is the official download destination.

How do I download and install it?

Open the latest release, download the APK asset, then open the downloaded file and follow Android's installation prompts. If Android requests permission to install unknown apps, allow it for the relevant installation source only if you trust the APK.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Pockeva is designed as an offline-first app and does not require an online account to manage your finances.

Where is my financial data stored?

According to the app's in-app privacy policy, financial records are stored locally on your device. Pockeva does not require an account and does not use analytics, advertising networks or telemetry.

How do I back up or restore my data?

Pockeva supports exporting and restoring supported app data using local backup files. Backups are generated locally — keep the backup file somewhere you trust.

How can I report a bug?

Please open an issue on the GitHub issue tracker. Including your device model, Android version and steps to reproduce helps a lot.
