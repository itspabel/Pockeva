# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Pockeva's issues and discussions to be a friendly, respectful place for everyone, whatever their background, experience level, gender, religion, nationality or ability.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give feedback that is specific and constructive.
- Respect other people's time. Search before you post and stay on topic.
- Accept that decisions about the app belong to the maintainer.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, threats or discriminatory language.
- Posting other people's private information.
- Spam, advertising or promotion of unofficial APKs.
- Any behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock issues, and block people who do not follow these rules.

To report a problem, email <hello@pabel.dev>. Reports are handled privately.
