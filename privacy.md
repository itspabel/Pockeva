# Pockeva Privacy Summary

This page summarizes how Pockeva handles your data. The privacy policy included in the app version you install is the authoritative document.

## What stays on your device

- Financial records, categories, budget calculations and shopping lists are stored in a local SQLite database in the app's internal storage.
- Profile information and the photo you pick through Android's Photo Picker stay on the device.
- JSON backups and PDF reports are created locally when you ask for them, and you choose where they are saved.

## What Pockeva does not ask for

- No account registration.
- No email address.
- No phone number.

## Tracking

Pockeva states that it does not include analytics SDKs, advertising networks, telemetry or crash trackers. It is designed to operate without an internet connection.

## Your responsibilities

- Backup files and exported reports can contain sensitive financial information. Store them securely.
- Local data can be lost if you uninstall the app, clear its storage or lose your device. Create a backup first.
- Shopping reminders depend on notification permissions and your Android settings.
